## What is LyricView?

LyricView is a fast, accurate audio transcription tool that extracts lyrics from your music files in seconds. Powered by cutting-edge AI, it turns any song into readable, copyable, and downloadable text.

No accounts. No subscriptions. Just upload and transcribe.

## How It Works

1. **Upload** — Drag and drop your audio file or click to browse
2. **Process** — AI analyzes the audio and extracts the lyrics
3. **Download** — Copy to clipboard or download as a text file

That's it. Three steps to your lyrics.

LyricView runs entirely on Cloudflare's global edge network, combining:

- **Cloudflare Workers** — Serverless compute at the edge
- **Workers AI** — Whisper large-v3-turbo model for transcription
- **Cloudflare Assets** — Fast static asset delivery

This architecture ensures low latency regardless of where you are in the world.
